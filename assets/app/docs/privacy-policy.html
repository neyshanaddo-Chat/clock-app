Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any information to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your settings, alarms (times, labels, repeat days), world-clock cities, timer and stopwatch state are saved only on your device so the app remembers them. They are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app asks your device to show alarms and finished timers at the times you choose. These are scheduled on your device only; no server is involved.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to alert you when an alarm or timer goes off.
  • Alarms & exact timing (Android): so alarms ring at the exact minute.
  • Vibration, keep screen on, run at start-up (Android): to vibrate when ringing, keep nightstand mode visible, and restore alarms after a restart.
