## Description (both stores)

Every clock tool you need, in one calm, simple app.

CLOCK
• Big, clear time and date, digital and analog
• Nightstand mode: a large dim clock that keeps the screen on

WORLD CLOCK
• Add cities from every time zone
• See instantly how many hours ahead or behind they are, and whether it's day or night

ALARMS
• Repeat on chosen days: weekdays, weekends or any mix
• Labels, four alarm sounds and adjustable snooze
• Shows how long until your next alarm

TIMER
• Quick presets from 1 minute to 1 hour, or any custom time
• Add a minute with one tap; keeps running when you leave the app

STOPWATCH
• Laps with fastest and slowest highlighted
• Keeps counting even if you close the app

MAKE IT YOURS
• Dark, light, sunset and high-contrast themes
• 12 or 24-hour time, seconds on or off

NO NONSENSE
• Works fully offline
• No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases
• No data collected. Everything stays on your device.
