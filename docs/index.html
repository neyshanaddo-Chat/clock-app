    How to use

    Clock: the time and date. 🌙 Nightstand mode shows a big dim clock.

    World: add cities to see their time and how far ahead or behind they are.

    Alarm: add alarms, choose repeat days, snooze or stop when they ring.

    Timer: set a time or tap a preset, then Start.

    Stopwatch: Start, Lap, Stop and Reset. Fastest lap is highlighted.

    Keys: 1–5 switch tabs · Space start/pause · L lap · R reset · N nightstand · Esc close.
